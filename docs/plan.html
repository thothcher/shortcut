What is built

Everything below runs at C:\Users\G\Desktop\konspekti and was tested end to end in a real browser.

For students

  • Georgian landing page with a catalog preview, price and FAQ
  • Sign-up with university and program, login, password reset
  • Library: their program first, search, program switcher, “continue studying”
  • Free sample books, paywall banner, reminder 3 days before access ends
  • “My subject is missing” requests
  • Account page: access, profile, password, devices, payments

Books

  • Each book on its own subdomain, such as cbt.shortcut.ge
  • Works with plain HTML, Vite, Next.js and Angular builds
  • GitHub Pages base paths (/CBT/, /body-lang/) detected on upload
  • Access checked on every file; a “ბიბლიოთეკა” button and e-mail watermark added
  • ZIP upload in admin, or an upload API for the PDF converter

Payments with Flitt

  • Hosted checkout in Georgian: card, Apple Pay, Google Pay
  • Signed callbacks; access added exactly once; refunds take days back
  • Status check with Flitt when a callback is late
  • A test checkout for development, no Flitt account needed

Admin

  • Overview: users, active subscribers, revenue, popular books
  • Books, universities and programs, users, payments, requests
  • Add days manually for cash or bank-transfer payments
  • Block a user or sign them out everywhere

Example

300 paying students bring about 3,000 ₾ a month before fees and tax. Fixed costs barely grow with students, so the real cost is writing good books.

Demand peaks before midterms and finals. Release books 2–3 weeks before each exam period.

Content and rights

Copyright is the main risk of this business

Selling access to a converted textbook without permission infringes the Law of Georgia on Copyright and Related Rights. It can also get the Flitt merchant account closed and turn universities against you. What is safe:

  1. Summaries written in your own words, following the syllabus. Ideas and facts are free; a book’s text, figures and tables are not.
  2. Your own flashcards, quizzes, games and exam questions. This is what students pay for.
  3. Lecturers’ material with written permission, ideally with a revenue share.
  4. Openly licensed sources (CC BY, public domain), with attribution.

Use the PDF converter to draft structure from material you have rights to, then rewrite. For unclear cases, ask an IP lawyer or Sakpatenti.

What makes a book worth 10 ₾

  • It matches a real course: the same chapters as the syllabus, and the course name in the title
  • Every chapter: key ideas, a short summary, key terms, 8–15 flashcards, a quiz with explanations
  • Exam questions with model answers, and visible progress
  • Accurate and proofread: one wrong quiz answer costs more trust than ten good chapters earn
